## 📋 Overview

**Recoveryfix Free OST Converter** helps users access and convert inaccessible, orphaned, or offline Exchange OST files into portable Outlook PST files. It runs locally on your desktop to ensure data privacy and preserves your original folder hierarchy and email metadata.

## ✨ Key Features

* **Free Conversion Limit:** Convert up to **500 MB** of OST mailbox data completely free of cost.
* **No Outlook Required:** Works independently as a standalone offline desktop tool without needing MS Outlook or Exchange Server connectivity.
* **Complete Data Support:** Exports emails, attachments, contacts, calendars, tasks, notes, and journals.
* **Folder Hierarchy & Metadata:** Maintains the original mailbox structure and email properties (To, Cc, Bcc, Subject, Date, etc.).
* **Pre-Conversion Preview:** View and verify mailbox items inside the built-in preview pane before saving.
* **100% Offline & Secure:** Processes files locally on your machine with zero cloud uploads.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Download & Install:** Download the installer from the official  [Recoveryfix Free OST Converter](https://free.recoveryfix.com/ost-to-pst/) and install it on your Windows PC.
2. **Select OST File:** Launch the application and browse to add your `.ost` file.
3. **Preview Mailbox Data:** Click on folders in the left panel to preview emails, contacts, and attachments.
4. **Convert to PST:** Click **Save / Convert**, choose **PST** as the output format, select a destination folder, and start the conversion.
5. **Import into Outlook:** Open Microsoft Outlook and use `File > Open & Export > Open Outlook Data File` to access your new `.pst` file.
